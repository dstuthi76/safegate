safegate

AI writes the bug. Gemini writes the fix. The scanner proves it.

Paste a public Next.js or Express + Prisma repository. safegate finds broken access checks and migrations that lock or wipe production tables, then asks Gemini for a patch and re-scans it before showing it to you.

    Repositories are downloaded to a temporary folder, never executed, and deleted within 20 minutes. Only usage counters are stored. When you ask for a fix, the one affected file and your Prisma schema are sent to Gemini.
